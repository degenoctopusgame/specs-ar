# Content and dependency provenance

## Created for Movement Club

The game state machines, procedural course/coach geometry, scoring, layouts and synthesized music/sound cues were created for this project with AI coding assistance. Audio generation sources are in `tempAssetGen/`; bundled audio is in `Assets/GeneratedSFX/`. The project includes two Park pace masters and six Dance tempo masters. No music, artwork or video was copied from the reference exercise videos.

## External dependencies

- Lens Studio and the standard Specs Base Template: Snap Inc.; governed by their applicable vendor terms.
- `Packages/SpectaclesInteractionKit.lspkg`, `SpectaclesUIKit.lspkg`, `Motion.lspkg` and `Icons.lspkg`: installed Snap packages, kept in their original packed form with package metadata. Existing notices and terms continue to apply.
- `Assets/AiPreviewAgentInspect_remove.lspkg`: development Preview inspection support used by Lens Studio MCP. It is retained because the submitted scene references it; it is not an original gameplay feature.
