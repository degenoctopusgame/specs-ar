## Description

Movement Club is a Specs Lens for hands-free spatial movement practice: Park Circuit, Court Coach and Dance Trail.

This demonstration shows the actual Lens Studio Preview using PC mouse simulation. Gameplay excerpts play at normal speed. The outdoor streetscape is Lens Studio's built-in Preview environment. Physical tracking validation requires AR glasses. Hardware comfort and real-world performance remain to be checked on Specs.

Park Circuit follows 20 rhythm cues. Court Coach practises seven destinations and recovery to centre. Dance Trail teaches an eight-count phrase at six tempos from 40 to 144 counts per minute. Every game offers direct start; Court and Dance also offer optional demonstrations.

Built with TypeScript, Lens Studio, CLAD, Lens Studio MCP, Spectacles Interaction Kit and Spectacles UIKit. The project currently passes 354 startup logic checks. Music and procedural gameplay geometry were created for this project.
